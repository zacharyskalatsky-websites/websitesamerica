Common Questions

Straight answers for clinic owners

What should a physical therapy website actually do?

Win the moment someone searches their pain, not your profession. People rarely search "physical therapist" — they search "knee pain when climbing stairs" or "physical therapy for sciatica near me." A PT site that ranks builds a page for each condition and treatment, answers whether insurance covers it, and makes booking an evaluation one click away.

Do patients know they can come to PT without a referral?

Most don't — and that's a marketing opportunity. All 50 states allow some form of direct access to physical therapy. A clear "no referral needed" message, backed by a simple explainer page, converts searchers who assumed they had to see a doctor first.

How do PT clinics compete with the big franchise chains online?

Locally, and by condition. Franchise sites are template pages with thin local content. An independent clinic with real condition pages, staff bios with credentials, patient reviews, and consistent local content beats a corporate template in its own zip code — that's exactly the work our program does every month.
