Why $10,000 when website builders cost $20 a month?

A DIY builder gives you software — you still have to design the site, write the copy, set up the domain, configure Google Business Profile, install analytics, and do the SEO. Our $10,000 build delivers all of that done correctly: website, domain, DNS, SSL, hosting, Google presence, SEO foundation, analytics, and call tracking, live in about 7 days. You're buying the outcome, not the tool.

Do U.S. veterans really get 15% off?

Yes — every tier, every price on this page. What's the difference between Growth and Market Authority?

Growth keeps your visibility compounding: five articles a month, monitoring, optimization, and a monthly report. Market Authority is for owning a category. We research your entire competitive field the way an investor would — verifying what every competitor actually offers, analyzing the live search results, and finding the claims only you can make — then execute with ten articles a month across strategic content clusters, new service and geo pages, and digital PR.

The program price stays the same — domain research and purchase is a small part of the build. If you already own a domain, we configure DNS, SSL, and hosting on it, and the cost that would have gone to a new domain stays in your infrastructure reserve.

What does the $2,000 infrastructure reserve actually pay for?

Hosting, SSL and security, domain and DNS expenses, backups, uptime and performance monitoring, and technical maintenance. Unused funds are never forfeited — the balance rolls forward, and once your site is established we expect the annual contribution to decrease substantially. Any expense beyond the reserve requires your approval first.

You keep everything. Upon full payment of the project fee, you own the completed website, the domain, and every article we've published. The monthly engagement has a three-month initial commitment, then continues month-to-month — either party can end it with 30 days' notice.

No — and no honest provider can. Google independently determines rankings. What we control is the work: build the site correctly, publish content based on what your customers actually search, measure everything, and improve every month. That's what compounds into rankings.

How fast will my site launch?

Approximately 7 days after we receive your company information, materials, and approvals. Delays in receiving information, account access, or Google verification can extend the timeline.
