Common Questions

Straight answers for treatment operators

Do you understand LegitScript requirements?

Yes. Certification itself belongs to the operator, but we build sites with the content LegitScript reviewers expect to find: clear ownership and licensing information, accurate descriptions of levels of care, real addresses, and no misleading claims. If you're pursuing certification, we structure the site so it helps rather than hurts.

How do you handle privacy on contact and insurance forms?

Forms are kept minimal and privacy-first: name, contact information, and an optional message — not clinical details. We add clear disclosure language and never store submissions on the website itself. For full insurance verification workflows, we link into whatever HIPAA-appropriate system your admissions team already uses.

What makes a treatment center website actually convert?

Families in crisis decide fast. The sites that convert make three things obvious within seconds: what levels of care you offer, whether you take their insurance, and a phone number that reaches a human. Everything else — photos, staff bios, accreditations — exists to support those three decisions.

Why should we trust you with a behavioral health site?

The people behind Websites America operate behavioral health properties themselves and run their own portfolio of treatment-education sites. We know admissions, payer mix, and what families actually search — because we answer those calls too.
