Common Questions

Straight answers for practice owners

What makes patients choose one dentist's website over another?

Three things, in order: reviews, photos of real work and a real office, and how easy it is to request an appointment. Patients comparison-shop dentists more than almost any other provider — your site has about thirty seconds to look current, show proof, and offer a next step.

Which dental searches are actually worth ranking for?

"Dentist near me" and "[town] dentist" drive the volume, but the money is often in specific services: "emergency dentist," "invisalign [city]," "dental implants cost," "pediatric dentist near me." We build a page for each high-value service so you compete for the searches with real revenue behind them.

Can you work with our existing patient management system?

Yes. The site links appointment requests into whatever you already use — online scheduling, a front-desk email workflow, or a phone-first flow with our trackable number. We don't replace your practice software; we fill its calendar.

How long until a new dental site starts producing patients?

The site is live in about 7 days. Google Business Profile improvements often show movement within weeks; organic rankings for service pages typically build over three to six months of consistent content and optimization. That's why the program pairs the build with monthly SEO instead of stopping at launch.
